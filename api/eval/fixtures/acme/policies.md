# Acme Analytics — Policies

## Satisfaction guarantee and refunds

- **Retainers**: cancel within the first 30 days for a full refund of that
  month's fee if we have not delivered the agreed onboarding plan. After the
  first 30 days, retainer fees are non-refundable.
- **Projects**: the 50% kickoff deposit is non-refundable once the discovery
  sprint has started. The final 50% is invoiced only on sign-off.
- **Workshops**: full refund if you cancel at least 7 days before the workshop
  date; 50% refund between 7 days and 48 hours before; no refund inside 48
  hours.

## Cancellation notice

Monthly retainers can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Annual
retainers can be cancelled at the end of the annual term.

## Data protection

- Your data stays in your own cloud account. We never copy customer data to
  Acme systems.
- We sign a mutual NDA before any discovery work, and a Data Processing
  Agreement (DPA) for customers in the European Union.
- All engineers complete annual security training. Acme Analytics is not ISO
  27001 certified.

## Intellectual property

Everything we build for you (SQL, dbt models, dashboards, documentation)
becomes your property on final payment. We keep the right to reuse generic,
non-client-specific templates.

## Service credits

Growth and Enterprise customers receive a 10% credit on that month's fee for
every support response that misses its target by more than 24 hours, capped at
50% of the monthly fee.
