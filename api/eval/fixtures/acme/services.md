# Acme Analytics — Services

Acme Analytics is a data and analytics consultancy headquartered in Pune, India,
with a second office in Berlin, Germany. We help mid-sized companies turn
scattered operational data into dashboards their teams actually use.

## What we offer

### 1. Data Warehouse Modernisation

We migrate legacy reporting databases to a modern cloud warehouse. We work with
Google BigQuery, Snowflake and Amazon Redshift. A typical migration takes 8 to
12 weeks and includes a source-system audit, dbt-based transformation models
and a documented data dictionary.

### 2. Dashboard Design and Build

We design and build executive and operational dashboards in Looker, Power BI
and Metabase. Every dashboard engagement includes a discovery workshop, three
design iterations and a recorded hand-over session for your team.

### 3. Managed Analytics Retainer

An ongoing monthly service where our engineers own your pipelines and
dashboards: monitoring, fixes, new metrics and a monthly stakeholder review.
Available on the Starter, Growth and Enterprise plans (see Pricing).

### 4. Analytics Training Workshops

One-day, hands-on workshops for business teams: "SQL for Analysts",
"Dashboard Design Fundamentals" and "Introduction to dbt". Delivered on-site in
Pune or Berlin, or remotely.

## What we do not do

We do not build mobile apps or web applications, and we do not provide
accounting or bookkeeping services. We are not a software vendor: we recommend
and implement third-party tools but do not sell a BI product of our own.

## Industries

Most of our clients are in e-commerce, logistics and B2B SaaS. We have worked
with more than 60 companies since 2019, across India, Germany, the United
Kingdom and the United States.

## Tooling we support

- Warehouses: Google BigQuery, Snowflake, Amazon Redshift
- Transformation: dbt
- BI tools: Looker, Power BI, Metabase
- Orchestration: Apache Airflow, Dagster
