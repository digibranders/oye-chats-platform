# Acme Analytics — Team

Acme Analytics was founded in 2019 by Priya Raman and Daniel Okafor. Today the
team is 18 people: 12 in Pune and 6 in Berlin.

## Leadership

- **Priya Raman — Co-founder and CEO.** Former head of analytics at a logistics
  company; 14 years in data. Based in Pune.
- **Daniel Okafor — Co-founder and CTO.** Previously a staff data engineer at a
  Berlin fintech; leads architecture and the warehouse practice. Based in Berlin.
- **Meera Joshi — Head of Delivery.** Runs every client engagement; joined in
  2021. Based in Pune.
- **Arjun Nair — Lead Data Engineer.** dbt and Airflow specialist; teaches the
  "Introduction to dbt" workshop. Based in Pune.
- **Lena Hoffmann — Lead Analytics Designer.** Owns our dashboard design
  standards; teaches the "Dashboard Design Fundamentals" workshop. Based in
  Berlin.

## How we hire

We hire engineers with at least three years of production SQL experience and
analysts with a design portfolio. Open roles are listed on our careers page. We
do not accept unsolicited CVs from recruitment agencies.
