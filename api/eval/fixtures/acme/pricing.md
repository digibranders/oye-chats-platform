# Acme Analytics — Pricing

All prices are listed exclusive of taxes. Customers billed in India pay 18% GST
on top of the listed INR price. Customers billed outside India pay the listed
USD price; no Indian GST applies.

## Managed Analytics Retainer plans

| Plan       | Per month (India) | Per month (international) | Included                                                                                  |
|------------|-------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Starter    | ₹24,999           | $299                      | Up to 3 dashboards, 2 data sources, email support with a 2-business-day response          |
| Growth     | ₹74,999           | $899                      | Up to 15 dashboards, 10 data sources, 4-business-hour support response, monthly review    |
| Enterprise | Custom quote      | Custom quote              | Unlimited dashboards and sources, dedicated engineer, 1-hour support response, roadmap   |

Annual billing gets a 15% discount on the Starter and Growth plans. Enterprise
pricing starts at ₹2,50,000 per month (about $3,000) and depends on scope.

## One-off projects

- **Data Warehouse Modernisation**: fixed-price quote after a paid discovery
  sprint. A discovery sprint costs ₹1,20,000 (or $1,450), takes two weeks, and
  the fee is credited against the project if you go ahead within 60 days.
- **Dashboard Design and Build**: from ₹3,50,000 (or $4,200) for a set of up
  to five dashboards.

## Training workshops

- ₹45,000 per one-day workshop for up to 12 participants when delivered in
  India; $549 per workshop internationally. Travel outside Pune or Berlin is
  billed at cost.

## Payment terms

- Retainers are billed monthly in advance. Projects require a 50% deposit at
  kickoff and 50% on delivery.
- In India we accept bank transfer and UPI. Internationally we accept bank
  transfer or card.
- We do not offer a free trial. See the Policies page for the satisfaction
  guarantee that applies to the first month of a retainer.
