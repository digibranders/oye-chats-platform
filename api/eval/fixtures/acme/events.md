# Acme Analytics — Events

We run free webinars and paid in-person events through the year. Dates are
given in the local time of the venue. Recordings and slides of finished events
are available on request.

  free). Daniel Okafor on BigQuery and Snowflake cost controls.
- **24 September 2027 — Live teardown: Looker vs Power BI** (online webinar,
  free). 5:00 PM IST / 1:30 PM CEST. Lena Hoffmann rebuilds the same executive

  one-day hands-on workshop led by Arjun Nair, limited to 12 seats.
